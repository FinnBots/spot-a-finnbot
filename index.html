How this works

Roll and spot. Pay 25 $OIL to start a round. You'll see a trait combination and 45 seconds to find the one matching Bot among 25 on screen.

Difficulty varies. Rounds are easy, medium, or hard (roughly 40% / 40% / 20% of rounds) — harder rounds have more decoy Bots that closely resemble the target. Which tier you get is derived from your entry transaction's own signature, so nobody — including us — can control it in advance.

Stuck? A 10 $OIL hint reveals one of the target's traits.

Bot-holder bonus. Hold a FinnBot and a win pays 1.5× the round's prize (Easy 100, Medium 150, Hard 300 $OIL before the bonus), paid in the next weekly batch — same as emissions, not instantly.

Payment. Each action (entry, hint) is exactly one signed transaction. Nothing is ever taken without your approval.

Wallet safety. We only ever read your $OIL balance and which Bots you hold. We'll never ask for your seed phrase.
